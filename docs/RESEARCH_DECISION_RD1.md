# RD1: stop the current fine-grained learned native control objective

Evidence anchor: completed PO1, commit `f109ab2`. This is a retrospective
research-decision synthesis. It registers no experiment, selects no successor,
opens no reserved panel and performs no new outcome analysis or model fit.
The [claim ledger](../data/research-decision-rd1/evidence-ledger.json) binds each
source to its SHA-256. The [revised manuscript](../paper/negative-result/main.tex)
is the current negative-result edition; the earlier `paper/main.tex` and every
other file at the anchor remain unchanged.

## 1. Decision and its exact scope

**Fine-grained learned native bitrate control is not currently identified and
validated as a robust improvement under this project's available fixture, data
and registered state-support assumptions. Close that active objective and
report the negative result. Do not choose another model or experiment merely
to continue the search.**

More precisely, no observed state has qualified the prospective reproducibility,
overlap and future-response contract required by the intended transferable
conditional-value study. The managed fixture does not expose the proposed
missing internal timing with both causal freshness and stable identity. The
instrumented alternative never reached build admission. The separately tested
PO1 history increment does not supply robust synthetic prediction, supported
action discrimination and useful calibrated uncertainty across held-out
families. These are different findings, with different denominators and limits.

This conclusion is **not a theorem of impossibility** for learned bitrate
control, all observable histories, instrumented Chromium, or all native causal
inference. V9 estimated local randomized proximal contrasts and V10 identified
a harmful whole-session policy contrast. Randomization can identify a marginal
policy/sequence effect without observing every hidden transport variable. What
has not been established is the stable, sufficiently supported conditional
value and risk needed to fit and promote the proposed fine-grained controller.
No absence of receiver outcomes in a support-only study means zero QoE effect.

PO1's stop remains binding: no history expansion, recurrent/sequence-model
escalation, refitting, calibration/threshold tuning, native factual-panel opening,
controller fit or safe-policy efficacy work from that representation.

## 2. How the evidence is classified

- **Supported:** a qualified, bounded factual or randomized claim in its stated
  panel. This does not grant source generalization or a controller admission.
- **Contradicted:** evidence against a specific superiority claim, or failure
  of an exactly registered operational recipe. A failed conjunction is not a
  statistical proof that every representation or intervention must fail.
- **Unestimable under registration:** a required acquisition, coverage or
  support gate failed, so the intended scientific comparison was not admitted.
- **Fixture-limited / not attempted:** required observations or practical build
  resources were unavailable. This is not evidence about the missing mechanism's
  scientific relevance.
- **Unresolved:** a broader causal, predictive or transportability claim was
  not established by an admissible study.

The native V9/V10 labels below denote the TURN/TCP clock/action studies, not
the older simulator studies with the same version numbers.

### Causal distinctions

M5 randomizes the early network-service prefix and holds the later **input
service** common. It identifies the total effect of early history on later
native BWE for the registered recipe. It does not separately intervene on
internal queues, estimator memory, encoder demand or TCP dynamics. Identical
later service does not assert identical realized sending or every path queue.

Matching on BWE or a post-prefix history vector conditions on endogenous
variables. Residual matched-context differences do not by themselves identify
a direct causal prefix effect, causal mediation or Markov sufficiency. Small
mean differences can coexist with large distributional/repeatability failures.
Some moving marginal trajectories look similar; that is not a prospectively
qualified stationary state, repeatable trajectory state, or action value.

Native BWE under a controlled synthetic service input is **native telemetry**.
It is distinct from simulated BWE. Conversely, replay of measured application
throughput is a documented local service intervention, not a live Internet
campaign or a measurement of every end-to-end capacity/queue mechanism.

## 5. Observability and fixture practicality are separate limits

**M11R:** both fresh native capability originals qualified acquisition, clocks,
startup, TURN/TCP, replay, readback and cleanup. Actual TCC, delay/loss estimator
update and probe events existed in identified RTC logs. Only1.78%/1.89% of TCC
events and 1.42%/1.77% of BWE updates were sender-available within 250 ms (required
≥90%);0/16 and 0/31 probe events were fresh. First identified export arrived at
about 59 s, then roughly5s batches. Producer timestamps do not backdate causal
availability. Fresh verbose target-update lines lacked PC/SSRC/controller
identity; post-stop tracing supplied no online sender receipt. Target emission
is not every estimator update. No equivalent relay/socket/RTT/RTP-burst proxy
qualified for actual TCC consumption or pacer/prober state.

Thus **0/2 capability originals qualified all required transport-control
channels**. The timing explanation of residual BWE divergence remains untested
in the managed fixture. It is not falsified by inaccessible observations, and
the presence of decoded events is not evidence of a causal online channel.

**IF1:** the pinned native-hook/identity/receipt plan was completed, but resource
admission failed. Audit-time free disk was17.7 GB, total filesystem33.8 GB, versus
the source's100GB minimum and IF1's130GB/16GiB/4-core admission. No qualified
remote builder or artifact was available. There were zero compiler invocations,
C++ instrumentation patches, full checkouts or new native sessions. This is a
**practical fixture-admission failure**. Instrumented Chromium's scientific
informativeness and live interface qualification were never tested. Static
hooks are plausible designs, not compiled or causally qualified measurements.

## 6. PO1 separates three synthetic failures

The preregistered necessary-condition screen completed816 new synthetic
episodes, three independently fitted seed batches and four validation families
disjoint from fitting/calibration families. There were 72 validation episodes per
family, six nested decisions/episode. All models/calibrators were locked before
validation decoding; all binary training/calibration support gates passed.

1. **Prediction transport:** H1 ordered history failed to improve over S1 fixed
   summaries in every family's BWE MAE. It improved on weaker persistence/current
   baselines in three families, so there is some information. On feedback-flap,
   H1 BWE MAE0.724 versus persistence0.407 is77.8% worse. S1 also fails there.
   This rejects robust transport for this tested package, not all history.
2. **Action discrimination:** counterfactual utility labels exist only in the
   simulator. H1 advantage MAE improvements over S1 were 0.19% and 0.43% in two
   families, below5%, with lower bootstrap bounds crossing zero and sign reversals
   across fits; the other two worsened. On feedback-flap H1 MAE0.225 versus
   constant0.080 is179.4% worse. No stable conditional action-value predictor
   was admitted. No learned action was executed and no native action value follows.
3. **Useful uncertainty/calibration:** BWE sequence intervals needed 4.08–5.40
   octaves (maximum 1.0); advantage intervals2.96–3.31 utility units (maximum 0.50).
   Aggregate sequence coverage passed, but widths did not. Every family failed
   the binary-calibration conjunction; feedback-flap ECE was0.397 for BWE drop
   and 0.321 for benefit. Zero supported action assertions failed the minimum 30
   asserted episodes/family; zero assertions do not establish safety. That
   family's zero unsafe positives also cannot establish risk sensitivity.

The PO1 simulator is one fluid/AckedBwe/assumed-encoder model with reused generator
definitions and fresh seeds. Its counterfactual labels cannot supply missing
native branches or resolve native identifiability. The64-session native factual
reservation remains unopened; **there is no PO1 native predictive result**.
The synthetic fixed 1.05 logger is not a retuned native baseline; native fixed 0.65
and the V10 closure remain unchanged. No interval/calibrator is repaired here.

Earlier simulator studies retain their bounded positive and negative findings
in the frozen earlier manuscript. Some synthetic population-panel criteria
passed while within-family calibration and comparisons with simpler predictors
remained problematic. They used different representations, policies and targets;
PO1 neither overwrites them nor turns them into native evidence. PO1 tests a
representation/model/logging/calibration package. It is not an information-theoretic
test that every function of observable history is insufficient, and its registered
stop prevents using that distinction to justify model escalation on this panel.

## 7. What remains unresolved

There is no established portable BWE-by-action interaction, qualified sufficient
observable native state, causal allocation of history effects to internal
mechanisms, qualified internal timing channel in the managed fixture, or robust
calibrated PO1 action discriminator. Whether an admitted instrumented fixture
would expose useful mechanism state remains open. Whether a *different*,
explicitly episode-level research question can be identified remains open.
No study establishes human QoE, live-network deployment safety, permanent
memory/nonsettling, or broad network generalization. Source reuse, repeated
landmarks, directions and crossed cells do not multiply independent campaigns.

## 8. Only two conditional future directions; neither is selected

The default deliverable is this negative-result paper and a stopped fine-grained
controller program. These are admissible changes in experimental capability or
estimand, **not an active follow-on queue**. No new allocation is created.

| Distinct direction | Evidence required before any new experiment | Evidence required before controller fitting is justified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. A genuinely new instrumented Chromium/WebRTC fixture** | An admitted builder or provenance-verifiable artifact meeting IF1 resource/effort bounds; actual hook implementation with stable PC/SSRC/controller identity, sender receipt, uncertainty/freshness/drop accounting and no future information. New fixture, not another proxy/feature on managed M11R. | Fresh technical and observability qualification; new untouched support panel passing the original applicable range/SD/overlap/repeatability and causal-availability gates; an independently preregistered causal contrast with replicated conditional action value/risk against strong fixed 0.65 across independent content/campaigns, and adequate calibration/uncertainty. Exposing counters alone does not establish sufficient state. Only that evidence could justify a new bounded controller protocol, not promotion. |
| **B. Change the estimand to complete prespecified episode/sequence interventions under partial observability** | A new scientific question and permitted acquisition protocol, independent episode-boundary randomization of simple fixed sequences, explicit initial/history-mixture population, content/campaign clusters, logging/positivity assumptions, carryover treatment, factual outcomes, strong fixed 0.65 comparator, risk bounds and precision/stopping plan. DirectionA becomes meaningful only with a changed resource/artifact premise;
directionB changes the question rather than pretending old state gates passed.
Randomization can identify a sequence-policy contrast despite hidden state, but
transportability still requires an explicit population and new evidence.
Neither direction waives an old gate, reuses a closed panel, authorizes a model
fit now, or claims that learned actions are better. If those prerequisites cannot
be met or the question is not valuable independently, stop with the current paper.

## 9. Paper and preservation disposition

and observability boundaries, and failure of a bounded synthetic uncertainty

distinguishes unsuccessful recipes from universal impossibility, and includes
