## Scientific decision

No channel met the frozen requirement for a repeated same-direction contrast with suitable controls in both contents. The assay does not establish an observable mechanism explanation or prospectively identifiable trajectory state. A trajectory-state efficacy study is not yet justified by this assay.

A new study would need a fresh preregistration and new captures, explicit causal availability, adequate repeated controls, and an independent demonstration of its proposed trajectory contract. The existing stationary/common-support gates remain binding for their stated target. No longer fixed wait, additional search of M9R, opened successor allocation, stability fit, action-value estimate or controller.

## Limits

Two reused content productions; one synthetic controlled input and one measurement program; only two development repeats/history. No new independent network family, population effect or broad replication claim.
