# M6R: history-state common support did not qualify

**The prospective screen is closed as a state-coverage failure.** All **32/32
original native sessions** and **16/16 suffix-fidelity pairs** qualified, but
only **four of six** frozen content/landmark strata met state-membership coverage.
The selected history state matched exactly the same sessions as instantaneous
BWE alone. Conditional future-BWE comparisons are **unestimable under the
registered gate**, and no history-state qualification is claimed.

M5 remains qualified causal evidence of a prefix-history effect on later native
BWE under its common measured suffix, with content/time heterogeneity and no
registered sustained convergence by 36 seconds. This study does not alter that
result, imply permanent memory or establish broad-network replication.

## Interpretation and next research constraint

This finite registered family did **not** establish a useful history-state
representation with prospective native common support. The selected addition
provided no matching discrimination at its frozen caliper, while the baseline
already lacked sufficient overlap in two fixed-time contexts. Because history
calipers are conjunctive, adding a coordinate cannot create missing BWE overlap.
Development score reductions that discard too much overlap are not a solution.

This is not a test showing zero conditional history effect, nor proof that all
observable history representations must fail. The fresh future comparison is
unestimable under the coverage gate. Matching is after the randomized prefix,
so even a conditional difference would not identify a direct causal prefix
effect or Markov sufficiency. No new causal action-value conclusion is made.

The next prerequisite remains a **prospective support design with adequate
observable-state overlap and repeatability**, using an untouched panel. Simply
trying a different state on these captures, relaxing calipers/counts, extending
conditioning opportunistically or fitting a controller is not authorized by this
result. The original multi-profile range ≥1 octave, SD ≥0.35, overlap ≥0.5 and
native repeatability/technical gates remain in force. V13 actions stay unopened.

For feature availability, see `NATIVE_HISTORY_STATE_M6R_FEATURE_AVAILABILITY.md`:
M5 lacks a regular sender packet-loss/received-byte history, but does retain
coarse phase-end receiver rate/loss snapshots. Those snapshots were not candidate
inputs. Relay forwarding/overflow are not receiver delivery/native loss or QoE.
Queue history is reconstructed from past owned-relay queue/service events, not
supplied as a future trace or capacity covariate. No receiver quality, pixels,
delivery/QoE outcome or bitrate-action outcome was opened. A future receiver- or
relay-assisted state would require its own causal availability/feedback checks.
