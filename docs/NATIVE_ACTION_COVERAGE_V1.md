## Independent fitting

`native-action-coverage-fit` requires both the original legal roles and their pinned augmentation parents. Full original and coverage raw replay precedes label loading. Reject copied sender/frame capture aliases, duplicate roots/steps, any role relabeling, action/readback mismatch or cross-role source overlap. New cap labels must be attributed to an actual acknowledged cap and be non-inflight; **startup misses stay in the labels/metrics**. Rebuild the 32×23 history from raw actual caps, causal ACKs and owned RGB.

The same three-seed bounded factual objective, physical-group-held-out training check, train-only normalization/weights and calibration-only exact-cap/context worst-group-out residual mapping are reused. New actions do not receive risk certification merely because their bitrate appears in a dataset: each exact cap/context still needs three physical groups, three episodes and 60 attributed requests, risk upper <=0.1 and disagreement <=0.15. The 95%-BWE limit and every original dwell/congestion/receiver-state/ten-millisecond/quality/deadline/family/high-phase/independent-validation/genuine-use gate remain unchanged. This is still direct per-request outcome learning, not a convergence proof or a learned long-horizon environment-return model.

Completed fitting output is `results/native-action-model-v2`; V1 is unchanged and `native_deployment_qualified` remains false. True-weight extracted parity and the legal recorded-input use probe now pass. Isolated local live integration is a separate prospective experimental step; selected-policy calibration/qualification does not automatically follow predictive scores or shadow activation.
