The externally accepted task/benchmark and final claim scope still require agreement and primary-source comparison.

## Completed delayed-return iteration: V11 rejected

`packet_transport._close` earns quality/latency/loss/deadline credit from a frame's capture-to-settlement history; `advance` averages only settled-frame rewards. One-step replay therefore receives mixed older/current-action reward, even though own-action effects can appear in later intervals. This is a hypothesis about learnability, not proof that n-step will improve performance or faithfully attribute individual frames.

Implementation: opt-in `TrainingConfig.n_step`, default 1, bounded integer 1–16. `NStepAccumulator` emits discounted control-step prefixes, uses `gamma**actual_length` for bootstrapping and flushes every truncated terminal prefix without carrying state between episodes. The default policy weights/logs are tested bit-exact against frozen V10 training for fluid and packet/history/demo recipes. Replay wraparound, terminal/no-cross-episode behavior, update-budget equality, snapshot isolation, strict config/digest compatibility and checkpoint/runtime paths are tested. More terminal updates per decision are not more total TD/expert updates: one replay emission per control decision is retained.

Development model seed **70**, trace ID **20001**: compare the V10 history/GCC-demo/cohort recipe with itself except **n=1 versus n=3**. Equal online episodes, demonstrations, risk/calibration budgets, architecture, exploration, conventional controls, physics and trace bytes. The audited development pilot gains 0.04962 stress QoE, loses 0.01528 nominal QoE and changes nominal/stress gated unsafe frequency by +0.104/−0.119 pp; both recipes have exactly 7,169 TD/expert-margin updates. This is descriptive single-seed development evidence, not significance or final-test results. The frozen five-seed study subsequently **rejects** n=3: validation stress-quality gain 0.00635 < 0.03 and score gain 0.00817 < 0.01. Fresh-test gated quality/risk differences are inconclusive, so the pilot improvement did not establish reliable benefit. All 792,000 rows, ten equal-update models and twenty intervals are audited in [DELAYED_CREDIT_V11.md](DELAYED_CREDIT_V11.md). No frozen guard is weakened after failure.
