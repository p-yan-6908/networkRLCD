## Why V4 is rejected

The ten V4b peers on one [460,480) s Tears of Steel clip and one 1.47679 Mb/s stable relay pass independent, read-only full raw/source replay. GCC's mean on-time fraction is 9.61%, versus BWE 80.87%, fixed-300 81.16% and bounded 82.18%. There are no relay drops; GCC sends about 0.95–1.01 Mb/s median payload but accumulates 108–121 ms cumulative native receiver buffering, versus about 38 ms for fixed-300. Requesting zero `jitterBufferTarget` does not force zero actual buffering. The low-cap controls encode only roughly 469–473 frames from more than 540 requests; encoder drops matter too. These are descriptive paired repeated peers, **not independent-group confidence or a universal causal estimate**.

All executed controls were exploration; there were zero actual learned actions. Shadow risks were generally too high/disagreeing, consistent with the original Sintel-only fit not establishing low-risk control on this clip. The actor is not rescued by calling fallback a learned decision. Full read-only diagnosis checks 301 retained files: `results/jobs/native-repair4-smoke-diagnosis.json`. Incomplete earlier V4 [440,460) evidence is preserved and never recollected. No V4 selected-controller/performance validation is allocated after this rejection.

## Independent V5 changes

- Restore **exact 85%-BWE fallback**, rather than unlimited 4 Mb/s when startup/feedback/congestion/model screens veto. Native GCC remains an explicit comparator; its ceiling is not a forced wire rate or risk certificate.
- Fix common sender `degradationPreference` to **maintain-framerate** for every controller, require native setter/getter reflection, capture that reflection in both raw streams and audit it. This changes the declared measurement recipe; old labels/checkpoints are not rebased into V5. It may trade spatial quality for fewer encoder-dropped source requests; live readback, coverage, quality and deadlines must establish whether it helps.
- Expire the RTT latency floor after four seconds and mask nonphysical zero-RTT validity in the explicit policy transform. The 30 ms rise alarm and two-second hold stay unchanged. This is a robustness correction to a lifetime minimum, **not an established explanation of V4b**, whose minimum valid RTCP RTT was about 54 ms.
- Retain the 32x23 causal sender/received-ACK/owned-past-RGB state, all seven factual risk heads, <=10% upper miss estimate, <=15% disagreement, <=95% current BWE, support >=3 groups/episodes and >=60 requests, freshness/startup/dwell, and >=5% genuine useful departures from **both** old BWE and GCC references.
- Replace noisy unsupported-action-max CQL bootstrap with **factual implicit Q-learning**: value expectile, Huber critic, bounded targets and smooth target updates. Whiten states using **train inputs only**, fuse normalization into exported affine weights, and keep portable runtime inputs unchanged. This is a new algorithm, not evidence that its real fit converges.
- Add factual fixed-600 exploration. Fitting requires fresh sealed V5 train/calibration recipes, >=8 independent train groups, all four families and seven caps, and **two movies in each role**. Multiple parents are clip-disjoint and group identities are manifest-namespaced; repeated peers remain one source/schedule group. Legacy, selected, repeatability, validation and test labels cannot train actor/risk weights. Calibration labels only fit probability/support mappings; selected-controller calibration must be fresh and cannot modify actor/risk/config limits.
