## Reporting corrections and clarifications

1. **V10 assignment versus endpoint:** policies were assigned for whole sessions,
   but the primary delivery endpoint uses six registered **800 ms post-ACK
   windows** and a 150 ms deadline. It is not a census of every session frame.
2. **Intervals and units:** V9 uses 11 complete scene-seed contrasts with
   unadjusted 95% t intervals (10 df); V10 uses 12 seed contrasts after averaging
   session repeats (11 df). M5 uses 21 randomized pairs per content, exact
   distribution tests and separate 10,000-draw paired-bootstrap 95% mean
   intervals. None supplies independent source-population uncertainty.
3. **PO1 assertion margin:** the registered advantage lower bound must exceed
   **0.02 utility**, rather than merely zero. The minimum 30 asserted episodes
   per family and Wilson upper episode-error bound ≤0.10 are explicit. No
   assertion passed; with zero assertions the recorded upper bound is 1.0.
4. **Coverage versus useful uncertainty:** PO1's empirical aggregate sequence
   coverage meets the **80% minimum**, and within-fit coverage meets the **75%
   minimum**. Nominal 90% coverage is not established in every family. Widths
   fail usefulness; binary probability calibration also fails. The previous
   heading could imply that coverage itself failed and has been replaced.
5. **Coverage denominators:** V11D has 9/16 hold-complete cells but only 7/16
   primary-PSNR-estimable cells, below 13 required. Its two descriptive campaign
   means use four and three estimable cells. V12 retains its failed media-statistics
   original. V13's four range/SD passes are separate from its one full-cell pass.
6. **Nested diagnostics:** M8's 64 plateau occurrences share 16 runs; M9R's
   3,200 queries share 64 runs; M10R's 96 overlapping contrasts and 400 queries
   share eight development runs. The 23 divergent M10R contrasts are among
   49 compact matches. M11R event counts are nested in two originals.
7. **Direct provenance:** initial M6/M7/M7R/M9/M10/M11 failures now have direct
   citations. M6's 252 clock-cutoff checks are explicitly not 252 native sessions.
   Each substantive manuscript block has frozen source locators and scope.

These changes correct the presentation, without relaxing any operational rule
or converting an unestimable target into a result.
