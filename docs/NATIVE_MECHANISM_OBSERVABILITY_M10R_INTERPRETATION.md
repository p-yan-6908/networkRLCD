# M10R: qualified observability, mechanism discrimination not established

## What was run

Scientific preregistration **`597f3eac529930a329dde9c4b017e7d9217bfe12`**; prospective
qualification decision **`bebd85eae29ee64446d9ce3ee9d16baa990f8110`**. Four fresh
qualification originals passed before the eight fresh development originals
started. Development is exactly two contents × two histories × two repeats at
1.4142135623730951Mbps common service. The 10s prefix, 24s initial, 64s common and
12.6s measured probe remain unchanged. No longer wait or successor allocation.

M10's earlier four qualification captures are separately frozen and privately
archived. They failed the unchanged 2000ms cleanup bound because the new bulk
mechanism return delayed cleanup bookkeeping. M10R moved that transfer after
measurement/peer close and before the terminal marker, returning a small receipt
over CDP. Fresh qualification supports the serialization diagnosis. No M10 run
was reused; its eight-session development allocation remains unopened.

Owned relay snapshots are nonmutating, with both directions' conserved queue and
service counters. Every online association uses a browser receipt fence and an
already received snapshot. Neither later-fitted clock markers nor native epoch
timestamps enter causal features. Counter missingness and invalid raw values are
preserved explicitly; receiver quality/frame outcome files remain opaque.

Native RTCP RTT and **RTT-measurement counter detection age** had 100% main-phase
availability in every development original. That is the qualified age channel.
`reportsReceived` was absent in all **35,432** development native records. RTT
and its counter were absent in 394 early records, retained as absent, and present
in 35,038. No missing value was imputed. Native transport-CC feedback generation/
arrival age, pacer/prober phase and congestion-window internals remain unobserved.
ICE RTT was recorded separately and never substituted for RTCP RTT. High coverage
of this RTT-counter proxy does not mean the internal BWE feedback loop is fully
observed. The owned relay also does not expose queues inside Chromium or TURN/TCP.

## No stationary-state positives

There were **0/400 future-stationary labels**, nested in eight originals. B0 and
D1 each emitted eight first tags, none verified; P2 emitted zero tags, so its
positive precision is unestimable. No stability classifier, regressor, state
selection or controller was fitted. The two-repeat, one-input assay cannot satisfy
the unchanged four-member, range/SD/overlap confirmation requirements even if a
favorable descriptive comparison appears.

| Crossed-repeat cross-history | 14 | 7 |

Seven divergent comparisons share the same prefix history. This demonstrates
remaining native/run variability as well as cross-history mismatch; matching
endogenous observations does not isolate a new causal history effect. M5 remains
the causal prefix-history evidence. M10R does not contradict it or establish
history irrelevance. In particular, the limited six-second summaries and their
fixed resolution cannot exclude finer dynamics, unmeasured internal state or
unmeasured transport queues.

## Trajectory-state decision and next question

The observed BWE paths rise and fall repeatedly under constant service. Some
future marginal distributions match, while none satisfies the original stationary
contract. The counter assay **does not establish a reproducible observable
trajectory state**: no mechanism channel met the preregistered requirement for
consistent repeat contrasts with controls. It would be premature to preregister
a trajectory-state efficacy/confirmation panel as though a representation had
been selected or validated. Do not relabel the moving marginal matches as
stationary support or relax the existing gates.

The justified next preparation is to establish whether supported native event
instrumentation can expose transport-CC feedback arrival and sender pacer/prober
or estimator-update events, with sender-clock availability and no receiver
outcomes. That would require a new instrumentation-only preregistration and fresh
qualification before any new mechanism/trajectory development. The exact missing
channels are named above; generic RTT-counter age is not a substitute. Any future
trajectory formulation must define an explicit causal information set, a
time-ordered future trajectory target and prospective repeat/common-support tests
on new untouched data. No such definition, classifier or new allocation is selected
here. Bitrate-action and learned-control studies remain closed.

Scope is two reused content productions and one controlled input in one measurement
program, with only two development repeats/history. No broad measured-network or
population claim is warranted.
