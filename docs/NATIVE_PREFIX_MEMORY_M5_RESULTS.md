# M5: qualified instrumentation makes the prefix-history effect estimable

The instrumentation qualification and fresh causal validation are complete.
M4R qualified **32/32** original sessions before M5 existed. M5 was then
preregistered at **`692656354c33c54e8c6cc9472cc321e0ea131895`** and qualified
**84/84** original sessions and **42/42** suffix-fidelity pairs. There were no
M5 failures, replacements, extra attempts, altered gates or subset estimates.

The registered 18-second prefix-distribution test rejected separately for both
reused contents. Later signed differences changed direction, and **neither
content achieved registered sustained convergence**. The prefix-history
question is estimable under this acquisition recipe. Native common support for
an action study remains unqualified. No predictor, controller-state redesign,
bitrate-action analysis or controller fitting was performed.

M3 remains an instrumentation-limited, unestimable experiment. No M3 result was
requalified or interpreted as a history effect. The failed M4 feasibility study,
successful M4R qualification and every earlier freeze remain unchanged.

## Fixed intervention and service fidelity

The 21 randomized adjacent ascending/descending pairs per content used identical
multisets of the first ten one-second source rates, with **14.112 Mbps·s**
nominal prefix integral. From source bin 10 onward, both arms replayed the same
original measured sequence. These are constructed early permutations followed
by a measured-source suffix. They are not wholly measured traces.

The qualified clock polygon mapped actual relay rate ticks into sender time.
Every run started the common suffix before sender 11,000 ms at every feasible
vertex. All 42 pairs passed the fixed [11000,T] capacity-exposure check at all
four endpoints. The maximum registered vertex-based paired exposure discrepancy
was **0.000946502 octaves**, below **0.01**. This bounds the registered input-
timing discrepancy; it does not identify a unique queue/estimator mediator or
make every microsecond of realized transport identical.

History endpoints stayed within the original windows. Maximum paired differences
in selected history-end time were 68.0/94.5/97.7/75.2 ms for Kampar and
78.0/75.6/81.2/90.6 ms for Volga at 18/24/30/36 seconds. These are descriptive
timing checks; no endpoint was moved and no extra threshold was introduced.

## Scientific decision and scope

The immediate instrumentation blocker is resolved for this panel, and the
registered history question now has an estimable causal result. Early service
history matters even after the later input sequence is held common. Its effect
is content/time dependent and did not satisfy the registered convergence screen
by the 36-second endpoint. This result does not establish permanent memory,
behavior beyond the observed horizon, or a unique queue versus estimator cause.

The native action-study prerequisites remain closed: the original multi-profile
range, SD, overlap and repeatability requirements are not established by one
suffix experiment. A next support-only validation would need prospectively
reproducible history-controlled contexts and the same gates before action-value
work. No state representation has been selected or redesigned on these results;
no predictor or controller is fit or promoted, and V13 actions remain unopened.

These are two reused licensed clips and one reused LTE measurement source.
No M2/M3/M4/M4R capture enters the estimates, and synthetic M1 evidence stays
separate. Delivery/QoE, frame-quality and action-value outcomes remain unopened.
The legacy `outcomes_read=false` flag refers to those outcomes; registered BWE
support responses were explicitly analyzed here.
