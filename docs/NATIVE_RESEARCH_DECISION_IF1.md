# IF1 research decision: instrumented-fixture admission and partial observability

## Decision rule, before build or new native data

Run the registered IF1 resource/interface plan once within its limits. Preserve
M11R as a boundary for the current managed fixture, not as evidence that transport
timing is irrelevant. Source-level access to a private counter is not proof of
live, correctly attributed, causally available telemetry. A compiled interface
also would not establish common support or action value.

If no admitted builder/artifact can complete the required native interface, or
if the interface/common-support gates fail, stop the fine-grained state-conditioned
route under the available experimental setup. Record exactly which stage failed.
Do not use a resource failure as an empirical statement about estimator mechanisms.
Do not fit a predictor, tune a controller, extend conditioning or add representations
to closed captures. M5's causal history result remains qualified.

## Pivot in the research question

Treat native bitrate control as partially observed. The history available to a
sender defines an information set; it is not automatically a sufficient transport
state. Two runs with that same information set can have different latent mechanism
states and future BWE. A future scientific target should explicitly integrate
over, bound, or declare uncertainty about those hidden-history distributions.

The next question becomes: **under a specified population of independent native
episodes and prior histories, which complete policy/decision sequence has robust
performance and risk relative to a strong fixed baseline?** This differs from a
claim of per-hold value at a reproducible instantaneous native state. It requires
a new protocol and data. It is not an action-study reopening in the managed fixture.

## What a future protocol would have to establish

1. Name a permitted new fixture, independently sampled episodes, content rights,
   measured-network campaigns, prior-history distribution and capture unit.
   Preserve controlled synthetic service, measured replay and native telemetry as
   distinct sources. Reused content or traces are development apparatus, not new
   independent replication.
2. Define the estimand for complete prespecified policies or sequences, with the
   assignment at an independent episode boundary. Treat within-episode estimator/
   encoder carryover as part of that intervention. No crossover washout claim or
   unvalidated per-hold relay attribution.
3. Specify uncertainty about latent histories and campaign heterogeneity; retain
   campaign-level estimates and sensitivity to history-mixture changes. Distinguish
   stochastic outcome uncertainty, finite-sample uncertainty and uncertainty about
   the target population. A pooled mean is insufficient for robust-control claims.
4. Preregister factual baseline and risk comparisons, meaningful effect bounds,
   sampling/coverage and multiplicity rules, independent content/campaign validation
   and a stop rule. Fixed0.65 remains a strong factual baseline; V10 stays closed.
   Any uncertainty model needs a separate prospective calibration/coverage panel,
   causal feature availability and positive examples. No closed-panel classifier.
5. Keep any simulator, adversarial history model or synthetic belief process
   explicitly synthetic. It may propose stress cases; it supplies no missing
   native counterfactual labels or native control-gain evidence.

No policy is selected here. Worst-case optimization, a belief-state model, chance
constraints or a partially observed controller would each need justified assumptions
and a separately frozen validation. This document authorizes none of their fitting,
promotion or action capture. A conceptual pivot is not a claim of improved QoE.

## What can change this decision

An admitted, provenance-verified IF1 build can resume its untouched technical
and observability stages, then a separately finalized untouched support panel.
The required gates stay fixed. Standalone libwebrtc can be proposed as a different
target, with its own clock, transport, media and acquisition qualification; it
cannot retrospectively qualify Chromium. New fine-grained native action work
requires positive prospective common-support evidence and a new causal protocol.

Until then, preserve the missing-state/identifiability limit and pursue research
design for partially observed uncertainty, without another feature-expansion cycle
or controller fit in the closed managed fixture.
